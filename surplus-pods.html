Introducing Surplus Pods

The AI-run, 24/7 store that lives inside the buildings where people already are.

August 2026 · RootHold

Surplus Pods is autonomous retail, reimagined for the places people already live and work. A beautifully designed, AI-run store — open around the clock, with no staff and no lines — dropped into the lobbies, campuses, and communities where convenience matters most.

How it works

Walk in, take what you need, and walk out. Computer vision and smart shelving handle checkout automatically — no scanning, no register. Every pod carries a curated set of everyday essentials, restocked intelligently based on what the community actually buys.

“Adventure. Delivered. Sustainably.”

For property owners

A Surplus Pod turns underused square footage into a premium amenity residents and tenants love — and a new stream of revenue — with zero operating burden on you.

  • 24/7 convenience your residents and tenants will actually use.
  • New income through a simple revenue share — no staffing, no inventory risk.
  • Locally relevant assortment, tuned by data to each building.
  • Sleek, on-brand design that elevates your space.
  • Sustainable, low-waste operations by design.

Where pods belong

Residential buildings, mixed-use campuses, offices, hospitality, and transit hubs — anywhere foot traffic and the need for convenience meet.

The platform behind the pods

Every pod runs on RootHold’s Physical AI platform — handling inventory, dynamic assortment, security, and the resident app experience — so the store gets smarter the longer it operates.

Tell us about your property and we’ll follow up with everything you need to evaluate a pod — amenity fit, revenue model, and install timeline.
