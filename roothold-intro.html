Introducing RootHold

Why we’re building an economic-development platform around a single idea — community — and belonging by design.

August 2026 · RootHold

Most development starts with a building. We start with a question: where do people actually belong? RootHold is a master economic development architect — we design and build the systems, campuses, and companies that turn land and partnership into jobs, investment, and community that lasts.

The idea

RootHold isn’t a single deal. It’s a platform. One integrated system in which goods land, are made, and are sold — and in which people live, work, and belong. Where most builders optimize a parcel, we architect an ecosystem: the operating companies, the infrastructure, and the partnerships that compound one another over decades.

“We don’t build spaces. We build systems that turn land and partnership into enduring prosperity.”

Why RootHold

Community is our DNA. Every partnership begins with people at the same table — municipalities, owners, operators, and investors, aligned around outcomes rather than a single transaction. We plan for the next ten, twenty, and fifty years, and we build the kind of places people are proud to belong to.

The campus, reimagined

Our flagship is a mixed-use gateway campus that closes the entire economic loop in one place: a Foreign-Trade-Zone import hub, light manufacturing, a headquarters, retail, housing, hospitality, and a network of AI-run Surplus Pods. Goods arrive, are made and finished, and are sold to the community around them — while residents live and work steps away.

The platform

RootHold leverages the combined strength of four operating companies — Empire Building Materials, Surplus Pods, Sweetkiwi, and Qulana. Each is a standalone business; together they form a platform whose parts reinforce one another, from supply and manufacturing to autonomous retail and consumer brands.

The vision

We’re building toward a network of 100 companies generating $1M+ in annual recurring revenue by 2030 — anchored by campuses that give communities durable jobs, a growing tax base, and a place to belong. That’s the whole point: prosperity that’s designed to last, and community that’s designed in from day one.

Let’s build something that lasts.

Municipalities, owners, and investors: bring us your land and your ambition, and we’ll design the platform that turns it into enduring community.
