Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 31, 2026  ·  RootHold, Inc. — Delaware Corporation

RootHold, Inc. ("RootHold," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit rootholdinc.com and any associated subdomains (the "Site"). By using the Site, you consent to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide to us directly, and information collected automatically when you use the Site.

Information you provide: When you submit an inquiry through the Site's access request form, you provide us with your email address. We do not request or store payment information, identification documents, or financial data through the Site.

Information collected automatically: When you visit the Site, we may collect standard technical data, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages viewed. This information is collected through server logs and, where applicable, third-party analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and determine eligibility for correspondence or allocation access
  • Send you editorial communications, investment thesis updates, and fiduciary briefings you have requested
  • Improve and maintain the Site
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Email Communications

By submitting your email address through the Site, you consent to receive correspondence from RootHold, Inc. related to our editorial publications and investor communications. You may withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us at privacy@rootholdinc.com or by following the unsubscribe instructions included in any communication we send.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Site may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience and collect usage data. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You may configure your browser to refuse cookies; however, doing so may affect the functionality of certain Site features. We do not use cookies to serve targeted advertising.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Email addresses submitted through our inquiry form are retained for the duration of our correspondence relationship and may be deleted upon request.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

The Site may load resources from third-party services, including Google Fonts and content delivery networks. These services may collect usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

8. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. As stated above, we do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@rootholdinc.com.

9. International Visitors

The Site is operated from the United States. If you access the Site from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction. By using the Site, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect any revisions. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically. Continued use of the Site following any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.

11. Contact

For questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@rootholdinc.com.
